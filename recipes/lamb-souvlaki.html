Lamb Souvlaki

Lamb souvlaki with marinated pieces of lamb, threaded on skewers, and char-grilled to perfection. I like to serve these with rosemary garlic roasted potatoes, a Greek salad, and pita bread.

    Ingredients

  • ⅓ cup olive oil
  • 1 ½ tablespoons red wine vinegar
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 ½ pounds boneless leg of lamb, trimmed of all fat and cut into 1-inch cubes

    Directions

  1. Step: Whisk olive oil, lemon juice, red wine vinegar, oregano, garlic, salt, and pepper together in a medium bowl. Add cubed lamb and stir until lamb is coated with marinade. Cover and refrigerate 3 hours, or overnight.
  2. Step: Preheat an outdoor grill for medium-high heat and lightly oil the grate.
  3. Step: Thread marinated lamb onto skewers, reserving any remaining marinade. Grill skewers until desired doneness, 10 to 12 minutes, basting with the reserved marinade and turning occasionally for even cooking.
