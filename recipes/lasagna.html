Lasagna

This lasagna recipe takes a little work, but it is so satisfying and filling that it's worth it!

    Ingredients

  • ¾ pound lean ground beef
  • 1 (28 ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 ½ teaspoons salt, divided, or to taste
  • ¾ pound mozzarella cheese, sliced.
  • ¾ cup grated Parmesan cheese

    Directions

  1. Step: Gather all your ingredients.
  2. Step: Cook sausage, ground beef, onion, and garlic in a Dutch oven over medium heat until well browned.
  3. Step: Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, tomato paste, and water. Season with sugar, 2 tablespoons parsley, basil, 1 teaspoon salt, Italian seasoning, fennel seeds, and pepper. Simmer, covered, for about 1 ½ hours, stirring occasionally.
  4. Step: Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles in boiling water for 8 to 10 minutes. Drain noodles, and rinse with cold water.
  5. Step: In a mixing bowl, combine ricotta cheese with egg, remaining 2 tablespoons parsley, and 1/2 teaspoon salt.
  6. Step: Bake in the preheated oven for 25 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes.
  7. Step: Rest lasagna for 15 minutes before serving.
